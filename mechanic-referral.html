Career Opportunity

Mechanic Referral Reward

Help us find an exceptional mechanic and earn R3,000

HELP US FIND AN EXCEPTIONAL MECHANIC

R3,000 Referral Reward

Know a skilled, reliable mechanic with at least 8 years of relevant hands-on experience? Refer them to OutLand Power and Turf in Jeffreys Bay.

R1,000
After the candidate completes 1 month of employment from their appointment date
R2,000
After the candidate completes 3 months of employment from their appointment date

Both payments require the candidate to remain employed and perform satisfactorily at the relevant milestone.

REGISTER REFERRAL VIA WHATSAPP ↗

WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled form. Complete the details and press Send to register your referral.

Who are we looking for?

An experienced mechanic with practical knowledge of 2-stroke and 4-stroke small petrol engines (lawnmowers, brushcutters, chainsaws, generators), and preferably motorcycles, scooters and ATVs. We value strong diagnostics, honesty, reliability and quality workmanship.

We welcome suitable candidates from across South Africa who are willing to relocate to Jeffreys Bay.

How it works

  1. Ask the mechanic whether they are interested and consent to being referred to OutLand.
  2. Use the WhatsApp button above to send your details and the mechanic's details.
  3. OutLand checks eligibility and confirms whether your referral has been accepted and registered.
  4. If the referred mechanic is appointed and meets the employment milestones, the referral reward is paid as described above.

Referral terms & conditions

  1. The referral must be received and accepted by OutLand before the candidate applies or enters OutLand's recruitment process. A WhatsApp message alone does not guarantee acceptance.
  2. The candidate must have agreed to be referred and contacted. Please do not share their details without permission.
  3. A candidate already known to OutLand through an active application, recruitment discussion or earlier accepted referral is not eligible for a new referral reward.
  4. If more than one person refers the same candidate, only the first eligible referral accepted and recorded by OutLand qualifies.
  5. The referral reward applies only if OutLand employs the candidate in the advertised mechanic position. OutLand retains full discretion over recruitment and appointment decisions.
  6. R1,000 becomes payable after the referred employee completes one month of employment measured from their appointment date, provided they are still employed and their performance is satisfactory.
  7. The remaining R2,000 becomes payable after the referred employee completes three months of employment measured from their appointment date, provided they are still employed and their performance is satisfactory.
  8. Approved milestone payments will be made within 14 calendar days after the relevant milestone, subject to the referrer providing reasonable payment details. Applicable tax obligations, if any, remain subject to law.
  9. Recruitment agencies, professional recruiters and anyone charging placement fees are excluded. OutLand employees directly involved in hiring decisions are also excluded.
  10. OutLand may request evidence of referral consent and may reject false, incomplete, duplicate or misleading submissions. Personal details are used only for recruitment and referral administration, with access restricted to relevant OutLand personnel.

Programme terms apply to referrals accepted while this programme is active. Any changes will not retrospectively affect an already accepted eligible referral.
